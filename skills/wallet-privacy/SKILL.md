---
name: wallet-privacy
description: Check how private or traceable an EVM wallet is with the Nymra Privacy Scan. Use when someone asks how exposed, private, traceable or doxxed a wallet or ENS name is, what links it to their other wallets, to an exchange or to their name, what its privacy score is, or how to make a wallet more private.
---

The Nymra Privacy Scan reads a wallet's public history on the EVM chains Nymra covers and scores how traceable it is, from 0 (exposed) to 100 (private). Its two tools are `scan_wallet` and `fix_plan`. Both only read, and they run on this computer.

1. Get one wallet: a 0x address or an ENS name such as vitalik.eth. The scan needs nothing else. Never ask for a seed phrase, a private key, a signature or a login. If someone pastes a seed phrase or a private key, do not repeat it: tell them it has now passed through this chat, so they should treat it as leaked and move the funds to a new wallet. If someone pastes an API key, do not repeat it and do not pass it to a tool: tell them a key belongs in the plugin's settings (`/plugin configure nymra-privacy-scan@nymra`) or their own environment, never in the chat, and that this one should be replaced with a new key at its provider because it passed through the chat.
2. Call `scan_wallet` with that wallet. A scan takes about 20 seconds to 2 minutes; say that it is running.
3. Give the result in this order: the score and its band, then the costliest findings with the evidence behind each, then what was read. Keep the tool's wording for every link between wallets: a link is a likelihood with its confidence (Likely, Possible or Weak, with the percentage), never proof of who owns a wallet. Never write that a wallet belongs to someone unless a public label in the result says so, and then say that the label says so.
4. When the result has no score (a contract, a service, an unused address, or part of the history not read), pass on why in the tool's words. Never make up a score. When the tool names a key of the user's own for the missing chains, give its steps as written: which key, where to get it, and where to put it (the plugin's settings or their own shell profile). Never offer to take the key in the chat.
5. When the person asks how to improve, or the score is below 80, call `fix_plan` with the same wallet and give what to do today first, with the points it wins back, then what to change from now on, then what is on chain for good. Keep the steps as written; they come from the wallet's or the name service's own help pages.
6. When the person asks where their data goes: the scan runs on this computer and asks public block explorers and nodes about the wallet, the way opening it on an explorer does; Nymra receives nothing. What they type in this chat, the wallet included, reaches the AI provider like any other message.
